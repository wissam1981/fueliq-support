Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open iPhone Settings → tap your Apple ID → Subscriptions → FuelIQ Elite → Cancel Subscription.
How do I restore purchases on a new device?
Sign in with the same Apple ID, open FuelIQ Elite, tap the settings icon and choose "Restore Purchases".
Is my data private?
Yes. Your nutrition, workouts, and personal data are stored securely and never sold. See our privacy policy below.
Does FuelIQ sync with Apple Health?
Yes. Grant HealthKit access during onboarding and FuelIQ will pull steps, active calories, and workout data automatically.
Why isn't AI Coach responding?
AI Coach requires an internet connection and an active Elite subscription. Check your connection and make sure you're signed in.
What languages does the app support?
English and Arabic (with full RTL layout). Change it in Settings → Language.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data.

Troubleshooting

The app crashed or froze
Force-close the app and reopen it. If the issue persists, restart your device or reinstall. Contact us with the device model and iOS version if it continues.
Meal scan isn't recognizing food
Ensure good lighting and a clear view of the plate. If still inaccurate, use the barcode scanner or manual search as a fallback.
I was charged but Elite isn't active
Tap "Restore Purchases" in Settings. If it doesn't resolve, email support@fueliq.app with your receipt and we'll fix it within 24 hours.
