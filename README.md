## About

A single self-contained support page (`index.html`) for **FuelIQ Elite**, the AI
fitness and nutrition coach for Arabic and English speakers. It lists the support
and feedback contact addresses and links to the privacy policy and terms of use.

> **Note:** The live support site, with its privacy policy and terms of use, is
> published from the [`fueliq-supports`](https://github.com/wissam1981/fueliq-supports)
> repository on GitHub Pages. This repository holds an earlier design of the
> support page and is not published.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Support page: contact, feedback and legal links. Inline CSS, no build step |

To preview it, open `index.html` in any browser.
